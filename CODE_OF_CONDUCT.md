# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience. Discuss code, evidence and tradeoffs without personal
attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats or publishing private information.
Do not spam, manufacture engagement or pressure people for stars.

Maintainer Jillur Rahman may moderate, lock or remove disruptive content and limit
participation to protect contributors. Report sensitive conduct through the
repository's private reporting channel when appropriate; do not expose a target's
private details in a public issue. This applies to project issues, pull requests
and other spaces maintained by this repository.
